Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

YouTube Upload is a private tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly to upload videos and read the name of the connected channel. Video files and titles come from the owner's own Google Drive and Google Sheet.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only inside the owner's Google Apps Script project. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube Upload uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
